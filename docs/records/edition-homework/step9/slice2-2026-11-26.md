# Step 9, slice 2: the corpus's two dialect hooks leave the product's sources (2026-11-26)

The inventory's E.2 (`docs/records/edition-homework/r3/research-step9-inventory.md`). The outside audit of the
slice is `audit-step9-slice2-2026-11-26.md` (written on the branch `claude/edit-ahead`, under
`docs/records/edition-homework/audits/`); its eight findings are fixed in the slice.

## What moved

| Class | Lines | From | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RawSqlBoundary` (legend-engine's H2-written raw SQL translated for DuckDB and Postgres) | 221 | `core/src/main/java/com/legend/edge` | `core/src/edge_harness/java/com/legend/edge` |
| `StableScanOrder` (the corpus's scan order made explicit) | 598 | the same | the same |

Their two callers were overrides on the edge's DuckDB and Postgres dialects in `PureText` (`raw`,
`scanOrderPasses`). Those overrides are now on the harness's subclasses of the same dialects,
`HarnessText.DuckDb` and `HarnessText.Postgres`, which the harness's two styles hand the planner:
`HarnessText.UPSTREAM` and `HarnessText.ENGINE_TESTS`.

Who plans in a harness style: the relational corpus (`MinimalCorpus`, `LeanSqlCorpusTest`) and the one core
edge test that sends H2-written text to DuckDB (`ExecuteInDbDuckDbEdgeTest`). PCT is not wrapped.

## What the shipped edge library gained

The move is not free for the product's jars. `:edge` ships in the server, and it now carries three things
that exist only for the tests' harness:

- `PureText.withDialects`, a package-private factory: one of the edge's two styles the harness plans in
  (`UPSTREAM`, `ENGINE_TESTS`; any other is refused) with another dialect of each database.
- `PureText$Redialected`, the private final class the factory makes. `isEdgeStyle` gained one `instanceof`
  for it.
- `PureText.DuckDb` and `PureText.Postgres` are no longer `final`. Their constructors are package-private.

Each is held by a guard, because the package `com.legend.edge` is shared by the shipped jar and the
harness's jar, so "package-private" alone admits every edge class:

- the factory's callers are exactly `HarnessText`, and its class is made in the factory alone
  (`PlanStyleSurfaceTest.onlyTheHarnessMakesAStyleWithItsDialects`);
- every subclass of the two open dialects is a member of `HarnessText` in the harness's jar
  (`HookRulingTest.theEdgesOpenDialectsAreSubclassedByTheHarnessAlone`).

## What the product lost

One hidden knob. A shipped server started with `-Dlegend.exec.engineScanOrder=true` used to add the corpus's
scan order to pure/v1 queries on DuckDB, and no longer can: the pass is not in the shipped jars. Nothing
outside the corpus's build sets that property (`spec/corpus.bzl`, and in code `CorpusOn` and `CorpusOne`),
and no user-facing page documents it (read by `git grep engineScanOrder`, 2026-11-26: beside those three,
only the harness's DuckDB, three guards, a corpus test's comments and program records name it). It fits the
user's ruling of 2026-08-29 that the scan order is for the engine's corpus only.

No shipped road loses the H2-text translation: a product door refuses raw SQL and inline seeds, and a
product query's dialect is always its declared database's, for which `raw` is the text as written.
