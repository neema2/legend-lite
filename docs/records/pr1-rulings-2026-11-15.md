## R4. A computed Integer beyond 2^53 in a mixed list's order: a known limit

- **The expression.** `['b', 9007199254740992 + 1, 9007199254740992]->sort()`: a mixed list whose integer is
  COMPUTED (not a literal), beyond 2^53.
- **Upstream's answer.** legend-pure compares the two integers exactly, so 9007199254740992 comes before
  9007199254740993.
- **Lite's answer.** A mixed list's numbers rank as DOUBLE (MixedEncoding's rank), so the two computed values may tie
  or order wrongly.

    55f).
  - A computed Integer is not known at planning. Refusing every computed integer in a mixed rank would refuse ordinary
    PCT cases.
- **The ruling.** Accepted as a known limit, not refused. It becomes exact with O2 step 3's kinds-kept carrier.
