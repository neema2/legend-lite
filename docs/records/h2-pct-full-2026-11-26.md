## The list operations (128 pins): lite's H2 dialect does not write them

H2 has arrays. lite's H2 dialect carries a list as JSON and refuses the list operations by name (`H2.listCall`;
docs/H2_BACKEND.md section 4). H2 lacks LATERAL, a correlated UNNEST, lambdas over an array and a map type. Split by
reading each test's Pure source (in PCT every list is a constant of the expression):

- **(a) a constant list of one scalar type, a one-argument lambda at most: H2 can answer today** (FROM UNNEST(ARRAY[...])
  [WITH ORDINALITY], ARRAY_AGG ... ORDER BY, the aggregates, CARDINALITY, ARRAY_SLICE, ||, SYSTEM_RANGE). 76: Essential
  21, Grammar 8, Standard 47. For example sort::testSimpleSortNoComparator, range::testRangeWithStep,
  stdDev::testFloatStdDev, maxBy::testMaxBy, greatest::testGreatest_Integer, forall::testforAllOnNonEmptySetIsFalse.
  Probed: 16 of 16 answered Pure's expected value (sort, max, average, stdDevSample, median, mode, percentile, corr and
  covariances, range with a step, reverse, indexOf, forAll, add and slice, find, maxBy, greatest of Booleans).
- **(b) a lambda H2 cannot run as one expression, or a map: 20.** Essential 17 (fold x9, sort with a comparator x4,
  removeDuplicates with a comparator x2, map keys/values x2), Grammar 1 (testPlusInIterate, a fold), Standard 2 (max and
  min with a comparator).
- **(c) not settled: 32.** A list of class instances (testContainsNonPrimitive, testExists, testFindInstance,
  testEqualNonPrimitive, testMapInstance, testInNonPrimitive, ...), a list of mixed types whose element types Pure keeps
  (testMixedSortNoComparator: H2 answered [-2.0, 1.0, 5.0, 171.0, 342.0], Pure wants [-2.0, 1, 5.0, 171, 342];
  testGreatest_Number, testMax_DateArray, testMode_Number, ...), split, the regexp list functions (H2's REGEXP_SUBSTR with an
  occurrence answers one match). Essential 11, Grammar 4, Standard 12, Unclassified 5.

## The other refusals

- **B, H2 answers and lite's H2 dialect does not write it (36):** bit operations (BITAND, BITOR, BITXOR, BITNOT, LSHIFT,
  RSHIFT answered -5&6=2, 14, 6, BITNOT(max)=min, 1<<62, 4), 14 pins; a time bucket, 11 (DATEADD(unit, FLOOR(DATEDIFF(unit,
  origin, t) / n) * n, origin) answered 2024-01-28 for 10 days, 2023-05-01 for 10 months, 2024-01-15 and 2023-10-23 for 10
  and 18 weeks from 1969-12-29); a date-time from its parts, 2 (a DATEADD chain answered 1973-11-13 23:09:11 and
  2015-04-16 14:51:59.999); '%.Nf', 3 (TO_CHAR(x, 'FM9999990.00') answered 1.34, -10.00, 1.5000, -1.1); a count past 32
  bits, 6 (DATE + n * INTERVAL '1' DAY and DATEADD(YEAR, n / 12, ..) answered 33803336-12-17, 236611261-10-03,
  1410404-07-12 00:00:00, 25489-04-20 03:12:00, 2407-05-19 19:15:12, 800002016-02-29; DATEADD takes an INT count).
- **A, H2 truly cannot (5):** base64 (TO_BASE64, BASE64_ENCODE: not found).
- **C, not settled (6):** an empty map, 3 (H2 has no MAP type; JSON_OBJECT() answers {}, but no JSON merge for
  put/putAll); a signed 64-bit hashCode, 2 (HASH, ORA_HASH exist, not Pure's); the minimal-fraction date token, 1.

## Defects of lite's H2 dialect (23 pins, each passes on DuckDB)

Under one block per class: the dialect writes a DuckDB function H2 lacks (MAP_CONCAT, MAKE_DATE, CBRT, REVERSE, ARG_MAX,
ARG_MIN, JARO_WINKLER_SIMILARITY, LEVENSHTEIN, REGEXP_EXTRACT_ALL: 14), or answers wrongly (rem of mixed numbers, a Boolean's
format, a Float's toString in exponent form x2, a parseDate offset H2 cannot read, regexpReplace replacing all, the 'g' flag,
rpad with a multi-character pad, ascii of the empty string: 9).
