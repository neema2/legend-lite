TimestampProbe.java, run 2026-10-09 on DuckDB 1.4.4 (driver 1.4.4.0). DuckDB's driver cuts a bound date-time to the
microsecond, even into a TIMESTAMP_NS cast; passed as its text, the cast keeps every digit and answers as the
TIMESTAMP_NS literal does. (Postgres: its literal writer cuts to six digits and its driver rounds a bound value;
PostgresArmTest's nanosecond case, passed as cut text, answers as the literal.)
