(DuckDB, in-process) and `//core:ts_scale_measure_postgres` (the embedded Postgres 16), both manual, in

## 2. What they say

- **F1, fixed: a basket of 500 overflowed the typer's stack.** The translator chained the members' long relation
  left-deep, 500 `concatenate`s; `TsLines.balanced` puts N relations together as a balanced tree, log2 N deep (and the
  spread's instruments and periods alike). 238b6921b.
- **F2, open: as-of is quadratic per line on every database without an ASOF JOIN** (Postgres, H2, SQLite, MariaDB,
  BigQuery). Postgres runs lite's correlated `MAX` once per left row (the plan: `SubPlan`, `loops=5040`), not per
  pair -- but each probe scans the line's materialized CTE, which no index reaches: 5,040 × 5,040 rows for one line,
  8.2 s, with or without the table's index. `LATERAL` is no better (the same probe of the same CTE); the hand forms
  are fast only because they probe the indexed base table. The fix is a different emulation, plain SQL: the two sides
  as one stream ordered by key and date, the latest right date at or before each left row a running `MAX` over a
  window, then an equality join on it -- one sort, O(n log n). Item 7b (docs/TS_DECISIONS_2026_11_07.md).
- **F3, open: on Postgres the 100 lines' query (428 KB of SQL) grew one backend to 11 GB**, and the session's memory
  limit killed it (`dmesg`: oom-kill, postgres, anon-rss 11,011,560 kB). Not repeated on a desk; the Postgres target
  runs no wide table and a basket of 100 (`ts.scale.wideLines`, `ts.scale.basket`). To find out: the planner or the
  executor, and what in the spread's SQL.
- **F4: Postgres is 20 to 80 times DuckDB's time on the rest**, a row store reading 10M rows: one line 3.6 s (2.9 s
  with the index -- its plan to read: the line itself is a 1 ms index scan), every line 60 s, 20 items 11.5 s, a basket
  of 100 past 90 s. Postgres's form of each word is the dialect's to improve once F2 and F3 are done.
- **F5: the SQL grows about 4.3 KB a line** (100 lines, 428 KB; a basket of 500, 334 KB): the lean-SQL work.
- **Not measured yet:** the tile's cold open with 100,000 points (B2's other half), with item 8 (the class page).
