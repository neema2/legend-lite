## 0. First: the edge's spelling hook

PCT's channel A runs through the product door with no edge (`pct/.../PctPlanRoute.java:45` calls `Execution.run`; its
errors reach Pure's matcher as `e.getMessage()`, `PctExecuteNative.java:191`). Pure's exact texts are to be the edge's own
SQL render target (Q15), so D starts with a **product-owned spelling hook**, threaded through
`TypedQuery.executionPlan`/`Execution.run` and `testplan/PlanningOptions`, implemented only in `com.legend.edge`, and
passed by PCT, the corpus (`spec/.../MinimalCorpus.java:598`) and pure/v1. The MIR is the same either way: the product
dialect spells lite's form; the edge's decorator rewrites the same typed nodes into today's Pure compositions for the
dialect beneath. Precedent: `Lowerer.withEngineText()` (`lowering/Lowerer.java:230`).
